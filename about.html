About the project

Hello There

Hi, I'm Muneer, a Computer Science student at Toronto Metropolitan University. I created Course Atlas after finding it difficult to understand program requirements, elective options and prerequisite relationships across different calendar pages. My goal is to give TMU students one practical place to explore their programs and make more informed academic decisions.

GitHub LinkedIn

Course Atlas is an independent planning tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Toronto Metropolitan University. Information is linked to official TMU sources, but requirements and course availability may change. Always confirm important academic decisions using your Academic Advisement Report, the current Undergraduate Calendar and your program department.

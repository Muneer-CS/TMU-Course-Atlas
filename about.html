About the project

Hi, I'm Muneer.

Hi! I created this website after finding it difficult to understand TMU's elective and liberal course requirements - what I could take, what I couldn't, and where each course fit. The goal is to provide one simple place where students can explore available courses and quickly see which requirement each one satisfies.

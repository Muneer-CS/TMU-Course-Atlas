# Collection findings

## Confirmed structural cases

The first official program-page review confirms that Course Atlas cannot assume every degree is a simple eight-semester list.

### Cohort-specific curricula

Arts and Contemporary Studies publishes separate curricula for students admitted Fall 2025 and before and students admitted Fall 2026 and after. The new curriculum is introduced progressively across later academic years. Its Liberal Studies counts also change by admission cohort.

Course Atlas must therefore attach curriculum rules to an admission cohort even though the public interface displays only the 2026-2027 calendar.

Official source: https://www.torontomu.ca/calendar/2026-2027/programs/arts/arts_contemporary/

### Co-op sequences

Arts and Contemporary Studies has both a four-year regular plan and a five-year co-op plan. Work terms change the academic sequence, and completion of published requisites does not by itself establish eligibility for a co-op placement.

### Level-based programs

Public Administration and Governance is published as Levels 1, 2, and 3 rather than a standard eight-semester sequence. Level 3 contains a Course Option and a Practicum Option, with distinct requirements and an employment and CGPA condition for the practicum.

Official source: https://www.torontomu.ca/calendar/2026-2027/programs/arts/public_admin/

### Grouped program pages

The Performance calendar entry groups Acting, Dance, and Design and Production. These must become separate selectable plans under one official program page rather than one blended curriculum.

Official source: https://www.torontomu.ca/calendar/2026-2027/programs/fcad/performance/

## Model decisions resulting from evidence

- The student selects a program first and a plan only when that program actually has multiple official paths.
- Admission cohort is stored internally and requested only when it changes the published requirements.
- A curriculum period may be a semester, year, level, work term, or another official unit.
- Co-op, practicum, partnership, and direct-entry conditions use explicit rule types.
- A future-year portion of a phased curriculum remains linked to its official source and is not treated as currently offered course scheduling.
- Liberal Studies and Open Elective eligibility is evaluated against both the general policy and the program-specific restrictions.
